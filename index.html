rclone personal

A personal, non-commercial application that lets the rclone client running on its owner’s own devices access the owner’s Google Drive to synchronize the owner’s own files.

rclone personal is an OAuth client used by a single person — the owner — to keep personal files (such as a KeePassXC password database) in sync between the owner’s computers and the owner’s Google Drive.

  • Used exclusively by the owner; there are no other users.
  • Runs locally through rclone on the owner’s devices; there is no server.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared or used for advertising.
